Tilecal for iPhone

Add a widget

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Tilecal and pick Tile, Flip-through, Today or the large calendar. Tap today on the widget to log it. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize → Lock Screen → add a widget.

My reminders don't show up

Check iOS Settings → Apps → Tilecal → Notifications: allow notifications and turn on Time Sensitive so dose reminders can break through a Focus. Reminders keep nudging until you log Taken, Skip or Snooze. Turn on Discreet mode in Tilecal to hide medication names on the Lock Screen.

Steps or weight are missing

Tilecal reads them from Apple Health. Open the Health app → your picture → Apps → Tilecal and turn on the data types you want. Apple Health on your iPhone must have that data (for example, steps from your iPhone or Apple Watch).

Line up a progress photo

In a photo series, tap the camera. The faint ghost is your last photo: line yourself up with it, keep the same light (the checklist helps), and take the shot. Use the slider to compare Day 0 with today. Lock your photos with Face ID in Settings.

Tilecal Plus

Plus adds unlimited trackers and photo series, the difference map and every hair zone, export (CSV, full backup, PDF report) and unlimited Connected AI. It's $44.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, $6.99 a month, or $99.99 once, and Family Sharing is included. Restore Purchases is on the paywall and in Settings → Tilecal Plus. To cancel a subscription, go to iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tilecal Plus, at least 24 hours before it renews. If Plus ends, nothing is deleted: extra trackers become read-only and everything stays viewable. For a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Back up or delete my data

Your data lives only on your iPhone (and in your iPhone backup). With Plus, Settings → Export saves a CSV, a full backup with photos, or a PDF report. Delete all data in Settings is always free and erases every tracker, log and photo from Tilecal.

Contact

Open an issue on GitHub with your question or problem. Issues are public, so please don't include photos, health details or anything personal; we don't need them to help. We'll get back to you there.
