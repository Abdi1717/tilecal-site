Tilecal for iPhone

Tilecal's Settings are behind the … button on Today.

Back up, export and import

Your data lives only on your iPhone (and in your iPhone backup); there's no Tilecal account or server. In Settings → Data:

  • Export (Plus) saves a spreadsheet (CSV), a full .zip backup with your photos, or a PDF report for your doctor. Save it to Files or share it wherever you like. If you ever had Plus, export stays available after you cancel.
  • Import (free) reads a Tilecal CSV or .zip backup, or a CSV from another app: match its date, value and name columns and check the first rows. Merge keeps what's already here and only fills in missing days. Replace clears the data on this iPhone first, and asks again before it does.

Moving to a new iPhone

The easiest way is Apple's: set up the new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup, or with Quick Start, and Tilecal comes across with everything in it. Or, on the old iPhone, export a full .zip backup (Plus), install Tilecal on the new one and import the file in Settings → Data → Import. Afterwards, check Apple Health access and notifications on the new iPhone (below), and add your widgets again.

Apple Health permissions

Tilecal reads steps, weight, body fat and waist, and the medications you choose to share. It saves the water, weight, body fat and waist you log in Tilecal to Apple Health. To change access, open the Health app → your picture → Apps → Tilecal (or iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Tilecal) and turn each data type on or off. Apple Health on your iPhone must have the data, for example steps from your iPhone or Apple Watch. If steps or weight are missing, this is the first place to look.

Add a widget

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Tilecal and pick Tile, Flip-through, Today, Steps or a Large widget. Tap today on the widget to log it. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize → Lock Screen → add a widget.

My reminders don't show up

Check iOS Settings → Apps → Tilecal → Notifications: allow notifications and turn on Time Sensitive so dose reminders can break through a Focus. Reminders keep nudging until you log Taken, Skip or Snooze. Discreet mode (on by default) hides medication names on the Lock Screen. For the dose countdown on the Lock Screen, make sure Live Activities is on in the same place.

Line up a progress photo

In a photo series, tap the camera. The faint ghost is your last photo: line yourself up with it, keep the same light (the checklist helps), and take the shot. Use the slider to compare Day 0 with today. Lock your photos with Face ID in Settings.

Tilecal Plus

Plus adds unlimited trackers and photo series, the difference map and every hair zone, export (CSV, full backup, PDF report), Night paper, insights and monthly recaps, and unlimited Connected AI. It's $44.99 a year with a 7-day free trial (about $3.75 a month), $6.99 a month, or $99.99 once, and Family Sharing is included. Prices are in US dollars and may vary by country.

Restore purchases

Bought Plus before, or got a new iPhone? Sign in with the same Apple Account, then tap Restore on the Plus screen (top right), or Restore purchases in Settings → Tilecal Plus. Family members get Plus through Family Sharing the same way. Redeem an offer code in Settings → Tilecal Plus → Redeem code.

Cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. In Tilecal, go to Settings → Tilecal Plus → Manage subscription, or in iOS Settings tap your name → Subscriptions → Tilecal Plus. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews. Apple explains it here: Cancel a subscription from Apple. If Plus ends, nothing is deleted: extra trackers become read-only and everything stays viewable. For a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Delete my data

Delete all data in Settings → Data is always free and erases every tracker, log and photo from Tilecal. Data already saved to Apple Health stays there until you delete it in the Health app.

Contact
