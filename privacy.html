Privacy Policy

Tilecal for iPhone · Effective October 29, 2026

The short version

Everything you put in Tilecal stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app doesn't send us any information, and we never sell or share it. On the App Store, Tilecal's privacy label is Data Not Collected.

Who we are

Tilecal is made by Abdinajib Hussein Mohamed, an independent developer in the United States ("we"). We don't hold any data about you unless you contact us.

What the app uses, and where it stays

Everything below is processed and stored on your iPhone and is never sent to us.

  • Your trackers and logs (habits, medications, doses, numbers, notes) are stored in the app's storage on your iPhone and shared with Tilecal's widgets on the same device.
  • Apple Health (optional). If you allow it, Tilecal reads steps, weight, body fat, waist and the medications you choose to share, to show them on your tiles. It saves the water, weight, body fat and waist you log in Tilecal back to Apple Health. It never writes medication data. Health data isn't stored in iCloud by Tilecal and is never used for advertising.
  • Camera and progress photos. The camera is used only while you take a progress photo. Photos are kept in the app's own storage with complete file protection (encrypted while your iPhone is locked). They are never saved to your camera roll unless you export them. Comparisons and the difference map are computed on your iPhone.
  • Face ID (optional) locks your photos or the app. Apple handles Face ID; Tilecal only gets a yes or no.
  • Notifications. Dose and habit reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. With Discreet mode, medication names are hidden on the Lock Screen.
  • Siri, Shortcuts and AI apps (Connected AI, optional). If you ask Siri, a shortcut or another app to use Tilecal (for example "How many steps this week in Tilecal"), Tilecal answers on your iPhone and hands the answer to the app you asked. What that app does with it is covered by its own privacy policy. The monthly request counter is kept on your iPhone.
  • Settings and preferences are stored on your device.

What leaves your device

Only what you choose:

  • Export (Plus). A CSV, a full backup with photos or a PDF report goes wherever you save or share it.
  • iPhone backup. Your iCloud or computer backup includes the app's data, under Apple's privacy terms.
  • Apple Health. Data Tilecal writes to Apple Health is managed by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Tilecal Plus subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment under the Apple Privacy Policy. We don't receive your payment details, your Apple Account or a purchase history linked to you. The app checks whether you have Plus on your iPhone, using the signed purchase record Apple provides, and sends nothing to us. Apple gives developers sales totals that don't identify you.

If you contact us

Support happens through GitHub issues. Anything you post there is public and is handled by GitHub under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We use it only to reply and fix problems, never for marketing. Please don't post health details or photos. You can edit or delete your own issues and comments at any time.

We don't sell or share personal information

We don't sell, rent or share personal information, and we don't use it for targeted advertising. App Store download and crash statistics that Apple shares with developers are anonymous and aggregated, and are only shared if you opted in.

How long data is kept

Your data stays on your iPhone until you delete it. Delete all data in Settings (always free) permanently removes every tracker, log and photo from Tilecal. Deleting the app removes its data too. Data already saved to Apple Health stays there until you delete it in the Health app. Copies in older backups follow your backup settings.

Your choices and rights

You can skip any permission, and turn off Health, camera, Face ID or notifications at any time in iOS Settings. Depending on where you live (for example California, the EU or the UK), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information. Tilecal doesn't collect personal information, so we hold nothing about you beyond what you post in a public GitHub issue, which you can edit or delete yourself.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Not medical advice

Tilecal is not a medical device and doesn't diagnose or treat anything. Talk to your doctor about your treatment.

Our website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We use no cookies, analytics or trackers, but GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to run and secure the service (GitHub Privacy Statement).

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here with a new effective date. If the app ever starts collecting data, we'll update this policy and the App Store privacy label before that change ships.
