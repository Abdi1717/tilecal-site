Tilecal

Tiny calendars for everything · for iPhone

One small calendar for every thing you're trying to do. Tilecal turns each habit, medication, number and progress photo into a tile with its own calendar. Put them on your Home Screen and Lock Screen, tap today to check it off, and watch the month fill in.

  • Widgets that do something: tap today right on the widget to log it.
  • Medications that nudge: reminders keep nudging until you log Taken, Skip or Snooze.
  • Numbers from Apple Health: steps, weight, body fat and waist, with a calm trend line.
  • Progress photos that actually compare: a ghost of your last photo helps you line up the next one.
  • No shame, no red: missed days are grey, and rest days never break a streak.
  • Private by design: everything stays on your iPhone. No account, no ads, no tracking.
