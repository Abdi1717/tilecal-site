Tilecal

Tiny calendars for everything · for iPhone

One small calendar for every thing you're trying to do. Tilecal turns each habit, medication, number and progress photo into a tile with its own calendar. Put them on your Home Screen and Lock Screen, tap today to check it off, and watch the month fill in.

A tile for everything

  • Habits: tap today to log it. Missed days are grey, not red, and rest days never break a streak.
  • Medications that nudge: add a pill, injection, drops or foam in four quick steps. Reminders keep nudging until you log Taken, Skip or Snooze, and "Took all" covers several doses at once.
  • Steps and weight from Apple Health: a calm trend line instead of daily scale panic, plus body fat and waist.
  • Water and your own counters: fill the glass by tapping or dragging on the tile. Pages, minutes, anything.
  • Number calendars: every day shows its value, and goal days fill in with ink.

Progress photos that actually compare

  • A ghost of your last photo helps you line up the next one: same lens, locked exposure and a "same light" checklist.
  • Compare side by side or with a slider from Day 0 to today, fade between them (Plus), or scrub through every check-in.
  • The difference map (Plus) shows, spot by spot, where something changed since Day 0. Hair mode covers the hairline, temples, crown, part line and donor area.
  • Photos never go to your camera roll. Lock them with Face ID.

Widgets and Live Activities

  • Tap today right on the widget to log it. Tile, Flip-through, Today checklist and Large widgets, plus Lock Screen and StandBy.
  • Steps widgets: this week's total, a day-by-day ledger and your usual pace.
  • A Live Activity counts down to your next dose, with Taken and Snooze on the Lock Screen. Follow today's steps pace in the Dynamic Island.
  • Discreet mode hides medication names on the Lock Screen and is on by default.

Insights, You and themes

  • One plain line under each tracker tells you how it's going. With Plus: insight cards, a Monday recap, monthly recaps and look-backs.
  • A You page with your totals: days logged, longest streak, trackers and photos. No account needed.
  • Paper and Night paper looks for the app and widgets (Night paper with Plus).

Fits your day

  • Import from other apps: bring a CSV from another habit or health app and match its columns. Import never overwrites what's already here.
  • Time zones handled: a day you logged stays that day, wherever you travel.
  • Night owl? Your day can end at midnight, 2 AM or 4 AM.

Private by design

Everything stays on your iPhone. No account, no server, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. Your Apple Health data never leaves your iPhone, and Tilecal never syncs it to iCloud. AI apps only see what you choose to share. The App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected. Read the Privacy Policy.
